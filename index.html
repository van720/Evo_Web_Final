In-House Design

One Team, From Concept to Completion

Evoterra designs the projects it builds. Keeping design in-house lowers risk, shortens the project lifecycle, and reduces cost — because the people drawing the plans are accountable for delivering them.

01

Reduced Risk

In-house design means no handoffs between disconnected architects and builders. One team owns the vision from concept to completion.

02

Faster Timelines

Design and construction teams work side-by-side, cutting the back-and-forth that typically stalls projects between design revisions and buildability review.

03

Lower Costs

Design decisions are made with construction cost and feasibility in mind from day one, avoiding the redesigns and change orders that come from siloed design/build teams.
